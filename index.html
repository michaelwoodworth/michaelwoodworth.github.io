Michael H. Woodworth

MD, MSc, FIDSA  ·  Infectious Diseases

Physician-scientist developing microbiome-based therapies to combat antimicrobial resistance. I lead clinical trials of fecal microbiota transplantation, direct a stool donor program, and integrate metagenomics with clinical research to move from pathogen-focused medicine toward ecosystem-level solutions.

Positions

Emory University

Associate Professor of Medicine, Division of Infectious Diseases. Medical Director, Microbiome Enrichment Program Stool Donor Program. Sponsor-Investigator for FDA-authorized FMT clinical trials.

MaineHealth

Associate Professor of Medicine with dual appointment. Engaged in clinical trial infrastructure development and translational microbiome research within the Northern New England health system.

Active Research
Clinical Trial

REACT

Clinical Trial

FAIR

FMT to reduce antimicrobial-resistant infections. CDC Prevention Epicenter-funded investigation.

Clinical Trial

APPS

Assessing the impact of peri-transplant antibiotics on the microbiome and patient outcomes in stem cell transplant recipients.

Genomics

Metagenomics & AMR

Strain-level genomic analyses of clinical samples to track antimicrobial resistance gene dynamics and donor engraftment.

Epidemiology

CDC Epicenter Workgroup

Co-chair of the Prevention Epicenter Microbiome Workgroup defining research priorities for microbiome-infection interactions.

Biomarkers

Microbiome Diagnostics

Developing microbiome-derived biomarkers for infection risk stratification, including metabolomics-based antibiotic detection.
